About this project

An independent computational biology project by Zoe Ng, carried out outside of coursework between grade 11 and grade 12.

Everything on this site was done from scratch: downloading the TCGA breast cancer cohort and its clinical records, assembling four further cohorts from public repositories, choosing a differential expression method appropriate to each measurement platform, combining five cohorts by rank aggregation, fitting the survival models, building a co-expression network from the tumour signal, and testing gene sets against it. The figures are drawn from those results; the site itself — the layout, the charts and the code viewer — was written by hand in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no charting library.

No part of the analysis was run for me, and nothing on the site is simulated. Where a result is weaker than I would like — the adjusted hazard ratio that lands at p = 0.05, the motility programme that turns out to be absent — it is reported as it came out.

Timeline

  1. August 2026 — scoping and acquisition

    Choosing the question and the target gene, learning enough R to handle a count matrix, and downloading TCGA-BRCA. Surveying around thirty public cohorts and settling on the four that give the most platform and ancestry diversity.

  2. September 2026 — replication and modelling

    Per-cohort differential expression, rank aggregation across all five, subtype comparison and the survival models. The co-expression network and its enrichment testing followed.

  3. October 2026 — write-up

    Exporting the figures, reading the published work on the same gene against my own results, and building this site.

Tools and skills used

R Bioconductor RNA-seq analysis Differential expression Meta-analysis by rank aggregation Survival analysis Network analysis Gene set enrichment Data visualisation Git HTML / CSS / JavaScript Scientific writing

The two that took longest to learn were not the statistics. They were deciding which method a dataset actually permits — a cohort with no raw counts rules out the model everyone reaches for first — and working out how to present a result honestly when it is weaker than the headline would like it to be.

Acknowledgements

The results shown here are based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network and by the investigators who deposited the four replication cohorts in the Gene Expression Omnibus. Every one of those datasets was made public by people who did not have to, and by patients who consented to their tumour being studied long after their own treatment ended. A project like this one is only possible because of that.
