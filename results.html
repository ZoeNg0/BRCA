Differential expression between subtypes

Comparing Basal-like tumours against Luminal A gives the largest and cleanest contrast in the cohort. The hormone-receptor genes fall sharply in Basal-like tumours while proliferation and basal-keratin genes rise, which is the pattern the subtype definitions predict.

Marker expression across all five subtypes

The pairwise contrast above collapses five groups into two. Reading the canonical markers across every subtype at once shows whether each group has the profile its name implies.

Survival association

A gene that separates subtypes is only clinically interesting if it also tracks with outcome. Splitting patients at the median expression of each candidate gene and comparing survival curves is the first, crudest test of that; the Cox model that follows is the one that actually counts, because it asks whether the gene adds anything beyond stage and age.
