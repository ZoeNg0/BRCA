The question

What I am trying to find out

Which genes most strongly distinguish the intrinsic molecular subtypes of breast cancer, and do any of those genes independently predict patient survival?

Breast cancer is not one disease. Tumours that look similar under a microscope can behave completely differently, and since the early 2000s that difference has been explained largely by gene expression: distinct intrinsic subtypes with distinct treatments and outcomes. Public repositories now hold expression measurements for thousands of tumours, which means a student with a laptop can re-ask questions that once needed a laboratory.

The question breaks into three aims:

  1. Characterise the cohort and confirm that the published subtype structure is recoverable from the raw expression matrix.
  2. Compare subtypes with differential expression analysis to find the genes that separate them.
  3. Connect those genes to clinical outcome with survival analysis, and check whether the association survives adjustment for stage and age.

Roadmap

Where the work stands

  1. Scope the question and read the literature Done

    Settle on subtype-versus-survival as the question; collect the key papers on intrinsic subtypes and the PAM50 classifier.

  2. Download and quality-check TCGA-BRCA In progress

    Pull expression counts and clinical annotations from the GDC, check library sizes, drop low-quality and duplicate samples.

  3. Exploratory analysis and clustering Next

    Normalise counts, run PCA and hierarchical clustering, and check the clusters against published subtype calls.

  4. Differential expression Planned

    Subtype-versus-subtype contrasts with multiple-testing correction, plus pathway enrichment on the resulting gene sets.

  5. Survival analysis Planned

    Kaplan-Meier curves and Cox proportional-hazards models for the candidate genes, adjusted for stage and age.

  6. Validate in an independent cohort Planned

    Re-test the strongest candidates in METABRIC so the findings do not rest on one dataset.

  7. Write up and publish this site Planned

    Replace the placeholder figures with real output, write the discussion, and get feedback before submission.
