Cohort composition

The subtype distribution is heavily unbalanced, with Luminal A alone accounting for roughly half the cohort. Any comparison between subtypes has to account for that: the smallest group has fewer than forty samples, which limits how much can be claimed about it.

Inclusion and exclusion

Samples are dropped for the following reasons, in this order:

  1. Not a primary solid tumour (metastatic and normal-adjacent samples removed).
  2. Duplicate sample from the same patient, keeping the one with the higher library size.
  3. Library size below the cohort threshold, or an outlying gene-detection rate.
  4. Missing survival time or vital status, for the survival analysis only.

Genes are filtered separately: a gene is kept only if it reaches a minimum count in at least as many samples as the smallest subtype group, which removes most of the annotation without discarding a group-specific signal.

Access, licensing and ethics

Everything used here is open-access and de-identified. No controlled-access tier of TCGA is used, no attempt is made to re-identify any individual, and no raw sequence reads are downloaded. TCGA data is subject to the NIH Genomic Data Sharing Policy and should be cited as the publication guidelines describe; METABRIC access through cBioPortal follows that portal's terms.
