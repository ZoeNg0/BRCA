Section 05

Discussion

What the results support, what they do not, and the honest account of where the analysis is weakest.

Interpretation

✎

To write once results are real. Structure it as: what was found, whether it agrees with the published literature, and what it would mean if it held up. Keep each claim tied to a specific figure.

Limitations

These apply regardless of what the results turn out to be, and are worth stating plainly rather than burying.

Association, not causation

A gene whose expression tracks with survival is not thereby a driver of it. Expression differences can be a consequence of tumour biology, of treatment, or of what cell types happen to be in the sample.

One cohort, mostly

TCGA-BRCA is a convenience sample with its own recruitment and demographic biases. Validation in METABRIC helps but does not remove the problem, since both skew toward certain populations.

Bulk tissue, mixed cells

Bulk RNA-seq averages tumour cells, immune cells and stroma together. A signal that looks like a tumour-cell change may really be a shift in immune infiltration.

Multiple testing and researcher choice

Testing thousands of genes and then focusing on the strongest inflates apparent effect sizes. Thresholds were fixed in advance to limit this, but the risk does not disappear.

Median splits lose information

Dichotomising a continuous variable makes a readable Kaplan-Meier plot and a weaker test. The continuous Cox model is the primary analysis for that reason.

Incomplete clinical annotation

Treatment details are patchy in the public clinical tables, so survival differences cannot be fully separated from differences in what patients received.

What I would do next

  1. Re-run the survival models with treatment covariates wherever the annotation allows it.
  2. Deconvolve the bulk expression into cell-type proportions to test whether candidate signals are immune-driven.
  3. Add a single-cell dataset to check which cell population actually expresses the candidate genes.
  4. Test whether a small multi-gene score outperforms any single gene at predicting outcome.
  5. Pre-register the thresholds and the analysis plan before touching the validation cohort.

What I learned

✎

To write: a short, specific, non-generic paragraph. Admissions readers have seen "I learned perseverance" many times; "I learned why normalising before clustering changes the answer" is the version worth writing.
